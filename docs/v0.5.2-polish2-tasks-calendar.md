# Study Hub v0.5.2 — Polish 2 (Tasks, Calendar, progress, highlights)

## Scope and data integrity

- Global Tasks uses compact actionable rows, with document/Focus shortcuts and the same viewport-aware contextual menu used by Course Study Tasks. The menu offers Edit, Plan or reschedule, Remove from Tasks where the item is week-linked, and Delete permanently with the existing confirmation.
- Upcoming separates work with a future **planned** date from **Unscheduled** tasks. A due date does not by itself make an activity planned.
- The Completed empty state now returns to Today's tasks instead of suggesting a new task.
- Calendar replaces redundant summary cards with a compact strip, keeps its month grid and drag-and-drop, presents only that day's planned and due work in the selected-day agenda, and moves undated tasks to a separate collapsible tray.
- The calendar date selection continues prefilling a new task. Dragging affects **planned_date**, not **due_date**. Same-day planned-and-due items remain single agenda rows.
- Learning progress now measures only activities linked to numbered teaching weeks (whether or not they appear in Tasks). Course-wide Exam Prep retains its separate revision progress; an empty learning course says "No learning activities yet".
- The redundant Exam Prep category helper is removed, with its existing revision-kind record storage unchanged.
- Selected, hovered and highlighted rows use the same comfortable interior spacing before and after highlighting. No database records were migrated or duplicated.
